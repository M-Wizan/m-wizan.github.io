I obtained my doctorate at the Herman Deleeck Centre for Social Policy, University of Antwerp. My research interests are wide and include: social stratification, inequality, economics of education, and more generally labour economics and social policy. While I initially have a background in economics, I engage in interdisciplinary research, drawing insights from sociology, as well as social and public policy. With my strong quantitative background and passion for learning, I am always eager to explore new methods and approaches to strengthen my research and broaden my perspective. 

In my free time, I enjoy playing video and board games, playing and listening to music (electric bass and trombone), and cooking. I also love travelling the globe together with my husband, and exploring different cultures and perspectives.
